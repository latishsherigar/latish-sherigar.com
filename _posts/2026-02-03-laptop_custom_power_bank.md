Worked with Google Gemini to build a custom power bank for laptop for 6-7 hrs of battery backup. 

Prompt to Gemini was to keep things simple, lightweight, portable and use parts easily available online. 

Gemini suggested the parts to be used and the justification..like the LiFePO4 battery rating, typ (fireproof, lightweight), a regular TypeC car charger from Boat, even the type of charger cable I should use. it did miss some things in the initial design e.g. a way to avoid deep discharge of the battery.

Although I have an electronics background but was impressed with the way it approached the problem. .. especially suggesting to use the regular car charger when I was thinking of a more complex design. 

Overall a nice learning experience.
